## Win condition and structural change

Establish early damage, make the opponent spend turns answering Highmane, and finish with Steady Shot, Kill Command and Bow. Against control, every unnecessary trade gives healing another turn. Against aggression, contest the board until our damage clock wins the race. Hero power offers repeatable damage after a clear, but is not fast enough to defeat unrestricted healing on its own.

Compared with the existing midrange Hunter, remove Krush, Rhino, expensive miscellaneous removal and weaker filler bodies. Add four real one-mana minions, Loot Hoarders, Tracking and Unleash. Keep Highmane as the top of the curve. This is a substantial curve/resource revision, not a claim to have discovered Hunter itself.

## Opening and resources

Seek Squire/Worgen into a useful two-drop and Companion/Bow. Crocolisk's three health gives Houndmaster a more plausible surviving Beast. Hyena is playable without a combo when tempo demands it; do not wait indefinitely for Unleash. Keep Houndmaster only with a credible Beast curve. Return Highmane unless slower matchup information and the rest of the opening justify it. Usually return reactive secrets and expensive/situational spells.

Tracking selects among three cards and discards the others in THIS pool. Choose the card that solves the current clock: early development when stranded, Highmane for pressure, Owl for a Taunt, or reach for lethal. It is selection, not net card advantage. Loot Hoarder supplies cycling; there is no strong bulk refill. Avoid spending Tracking's mana when it would prevent the necessary current play.

## Important sequences

- Kill Command deals five only while a friendly Beast is on the board. Keep a Beast alive until after casting; do not trade away the last one first. Two active Kill Commands plus hero power cost eight and deal 12. If no Beast survives, Unleash plus both Commands costs nine and deals ten spell damage, but only if the opponent has a minion so a Hound is actually summoned and board space exists.
- Unleash exploits wide enemy boards. Hyena plus Unleash costs five, but the new Hyena cannot attack that turn. Hound trades must actually kill friendly Beasts to grow it. Keep space for Hounds and account for Taunts.
- Highmane spreads its stats across deathrattle bodies, but is not immune to clears. Equality followed by Consecration kills the original and generally leaves newly summoned Hyenas; further damage can remove them. Silence, transform and stealing effects can defeat the persistence plan.
- Houndmaster plus a fresh two-mana Beast costs six. The buffed Beast does not gain Charge. Do not reserve Houndmaster forever if a plain body is the only useful development.
- Owl can open attacks through Tirion while denying the deathrattle. Freezing Trap instead returns a minion and does not permanently remove its future battlecry or threat; small enemy attackers can consume it first.
- Bow gains durability when our secret is REVEALED, not when it is played. An already broken Bow cannot be extended afterward. Avoid preserving its last charge when using it gives lethal or prevents a decisive threat.

## Matchups and weaknesses

Control Paladin: pressure with a few meaningful threats, reserve the next wave, use Highmane to tax removal, and finish in a burst window before healing. Zoo/aggro Paladin: Unleash and Explosive Trap offer recovery, but drawing only single-target damage can lose the board. Mage: clear high-value engines when necessary, but do not trade until our hand is empty; cheap minions and Loot are vulnerable to pings.

Eight starting minion cards are Beasts (Crocolisks, Hyenas, Owl, Tiger and Highmanes); Companion and Unleash add more. Squire/Worgen/Loot improve early consistency without activating Kill Command or Houndmaster. That tension must be measured. The deck has no healing and limited refill, so an early failed race can be unrecoverable.

First isolated adjustments: Tiger → second Owl if Taunts repeatedly lock lethal; one Tracking → another early minion if selection frequently costs a necessary tempo play; one Hyena → a second Tiger only if control value is the problem and early survival is already adequate. Report damage lost to missing Beasts and stranded Houndmasters separately from ordinary bad draws. Do not assume all three substitutions help together.
