# Control Paladin: Deck Guide (hs-arena, Basic + Classic Legacy pool)

## 1. Overview and win condition

Control Paladin is a reactive deck. It spends turns 1–6 removing whatever the opponent puts on the board
(Wild Pyromancer, Consecration, Aldor Peacekeeper, Truesilver Champion, Equality), plays sticky or Taunt minions
that trade well (Harvest Golem, Sen'jin Shieldmasta, Sunwalker, Cairne Bloodhoof), keeps its own health high with
heals (Earthen Ring Farseer, Truesilver, Darkscale Healer, Guardian of Kings, Lay on Hands), and wins from turn 8 on
when the opponent has run out of cards: Tirion Fordring, Alexstrasza, Sunwalker and Cairne are bigger than anything an
aggro or midrange deck can still answer.

**Win condition:** survive to turn 8+ with a stable board and more cards in hand than the opponent, then land
threats one per turn (Tirion → Alexstrasza → Cairne/Sunwalker) and attack with them. You do not race. You win by
being the last player with minions on the board.

**Why this deck in this pool:** classmate decks in this pool are mostly board-centric aggro and midrange decks
(Zoo, Midrange Hunter, Aggro Warrior, Midrange Shaman, Tempo/Greedy Mage). The nerfs hit them hard (Leper Gnome 1/1,
Knife Juggler 2/2, Leeroy and Doomguard removed, Buzzard 5 mana), so they deal less burst damage than the 2014
versions, and our AoE plus Taunts plus heals line up well against them.

**Simplicity choices (close calls decided in favour of an easy pilot):**
- No Doomsayer (it kills our own board and needs planning around its timing).
- No Defender of Argus / Sunfury Protector (positional), no Redemption / secrets, no Blessing of Kings.
- No Humility: Aldor Peacekeeper does the same job with a body attached.
- No Big Game Hunter: in this pool it is 5 mana, and few aggro/midrange decks run 7+ Attack minions.
- Alexstrasza over Ysera: Ysera's random Dream cards add decisions; Alexstrasza has one simple rule.

## 3. How the nerfs and the Hall of Fame changed the deck

| Card / change | 2014 or online version | This pool | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equality | 2 mana | **4 mana** | Pyromancer + Equality is a **6-mana** combo (turn 6, not turn 4). Equality + Consecration is **8 mana**. Equality + Avenging Wrath is 10. Never plan the turn-4 clear older guides describe. |
| Consecration, Hammer of Wrath | 3 mana in newer Core lists | **4 mana** each | Turn 4 is crowded (9 four-drops). Pick one per turn; turn 5 often = 4-drop + nothing, so use Reinforce or hold. |
| Acolyte of Pain, Azure Drake, Sylvanas, Ragnaros, Mind Control Tech, Spellbreaker, Divine Favor, Leeroy | Core of 2014 Control/Shockadin lists | **Removed (Hall of Fame)** | Card draw comes from Loot Hoarder, Hammer of Wrath, Lay on Hands. Late threats are Tirion, Alexstrasza, Cairne, Sunwalker. |
| Haunted Creeper, Sludge Belcher, Loatheb, Antique Healbot, Coghammer | Naxx/GvG cards in 2014 lists | Not in pool | Replaced by Harvest Golem, Sen'jin, Earthen Ring Farseer, Darkscale Healer. |
| Big Game Hunter | 3 mana | **5 mana** | Cut. |
| Knife Juggler 2/2, Leper Gnome 1/1, Abusive Sergeant 1/1, Starving Buzzard 5, Unleash the Hounds 3 | Stronger aggro tools | Weaker | Aggro decks have less reach. Pyromancer pings and Consecration kill more of their board than they did in 2014. |
| Mage: Ice Lance, Ice Block removed; Mana Wyrm 1/3 | Freeze Mage existed | Gone | Mage opponents are midrange/tempo. Still watch Polymorph and Mirror Entity/Counterspell. |
| Force of Nature (no Charge), Savage Roar 3 | Druid burst | Smaller burst | Druid can still Savage Roar a wide board: clear wide boards early. |

The vS Data Reaper (2021 Classic, original cards) rated Control Paladin weak on the human ladder; it was
"fine against faster decks, struggles vs slower ones" (HSTopDecks). That is exactly why it fits here: the
expected opponents are fast and board-centric, and the slow decks that beat it (Control Warrior, Control Priest,
Freeze Mage, Handlock) lost key cards in this pool.

## 4. Mulligan

You see 3 cards going first, 4 going second (The Coin is added after mulligan). The opponent's hero class is
usually visible in the mulligan state; use it for conditional keeps.

**Always keep:** Wild Pyromancer `NEW1_020`, Acidic Swamp Ooze `EX1_066`, Loot Hoarder `EX1_096`,
Aldor Peacekeeper `EX1_382`, Harvest Golem `EX1_556`, Earthen Ring Farseer `CS2_117`.

**Conditional keeps:**
- Truesilver Champion `CS2_097`: keep if you also hold a 2- or 3-drop, or if going second. Keep only one.
- Consecration `CS2_093`: keep one vs Warlock, Hunter, Paladin, Shaman, Druid, Rogue (wide-board classes) if you
  also hold a 2- or 3-drop. Toss vs Mage/Warrior/Priest unless going second.
- Sen'jin Shieldmasta `CS2_179`: keep one when going second.
- Going second: keep at most **one** 4-drop in total (Truesilver > Consecration > Sen'jin).

**Always toss:** Equality, Hammer of Wrath, and every card costing 5+ (Kodo, Darkscale, Sunwalker, Cairne,
Avenging Wrath, Guardian of Kings, Lay on Hands, Tirion, Alexstrasza).

Second copies: keep at most two cards of the same cost; with two 2-drops and a 3-drop you have a perfect start.

## 5. Turn-by-turn game plan (ideal curve)

| Turn | Ideal play | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing (no 1-drops) | End turn. With The Coin, do not coin out a 2-drop on turn 1 unless it is Ooze vs a turn-1 weapon. |
| 2 | Wild Pyromancer / Loot Hoarder / Ooze, else Reinforce | Prefer Loot Hoarder or Ooze on turn 2; save Pyromancer if you hold a spell for turn 3–4 (a Pyro that survives is AoE next turn). |
| 3 | Aldor (on their best attacker) / Harvest Golem / Farseer | Coin + 4-drop is fine when they have 2+ minions (Consecration) or a weapon is needed (Truesilver). |
| 4 | Consecration vs 2+ small minions; Truesilver vs one minion; Sen'jin when the board is empty or even | Pyromancer already on board + Consecration = 3 damage to each enemy minion. |
| 5 | 4-drop + nothing, or Kodo / Darkscale; Pyromancer + Reinforce + ... | Kodo when an enemy minion has ≤2 Attack. 2-drop + 3-drop is a fine turn 5. |
| 6 | **Wild Pyromancer + Equality** (board clear) when they have 2+ minions with more total stats than yours; else Sunwalker / Cairne / Avenging Wrath | Pyro first, then Equality: every minion goes to 1 Health and the Pyro trigger kills all of them. |
| 7 | Guardian of Kings, or 6-drop + nothing, or 3+4 | Use Reinforce with 2 leftover mana. |
| 8 | Tirion Fordring, or **Equality + Consecration** (full clear), or Lay on Hands when low | Play Tirion when the opponent has used Polymorph/Hex/Assassinate or after Sunwalker drew it out. |
| 9 | Alexstrasza, or 8-drop + Reinforce | See Alexstrasza rule below. |
| 10 | Two threats, or threat + removal, or Equality + Avenging Wrath | Keep spending all mana. |

## 6. Card-by-card notes

- **Wild Pyromancer (`NEW1_020`)**: the trigger fires **after** you cast a spell and hits **all** minions,
  including your own and itself. Play it **before** the spell in the same turn. It pops Divine Shields
  (yours too: Sunwalker, Tirion). Good spells to fire it: Equality (kills everything without Divine Shield),
  Consecration (3 total damage to enemy minions), Hammer of Wrath. Do not cast spells with Pyro on board when the
  ping would kill more of your minions than theirs.
- **Acidic Swamp Ooze (`EX1_066`)**: hold it when the opponent's class uses weapons (Warrior, Rogue, Hunter,
  Shaman, Paladin) and they have none yet, unless you have no other turn-2 play. Play it immediately when they equip
  Fiery War Axe, Arcanite Reaper, Truesilver, Eaglehorn Bow, Gladiator's Longbow, Doomhammer, Assassin's Blade.
  Vs Mage, Priest, Druid, Warlock it is just a 3/2.
- **Loot Hoarder (`EX1_096`)**: turn-2 play; trade it into anything, the draw is the point.
- **Aldor Peacekeeper (`EX1_382`)**: target = enemy minion with the **highest Attack** (ties: higher Health, then
  Taunt). Best on 5+ Attack minions (Boulderfist Ogre, Stranglethorn Tiger, Core Hound, Grommash, Ancient of War,
  Savannah Highmane, Fire Elemental). On turn 3 vs aggro, use it on their best 3+ Attack minion.
- **Harvest Golem (`EX1_556`)**: plain sticky 3-drop. Good into Pyromancer/Consecration turns because the 2/1 stays.
- **Earthen Ring Farseer (`CS2_117`)**: heal target = your hero if it is missing 3+ Health, else your most valuable
  damaged minion, else your hero.
- **Equality (`EX1_619`)**: sets **all** minions (both sides) to 1 Health. Only cast it with a follow-up the same
  turn that kills the 1-Health enemies: Wild Pyromancer on board (cast Pyro first), Consecration, Avenging Wrath, or
  enough attackers. Cast when the enemy board is worth clearly more than yours (two or more enemy minions, or one
  enemy minion with 6+ Health you can't otherwise answer, killed by an attack after Equality).
- **Consecration (`CS2_093`)**: cast when it kills 2+ enemy minions, or kills 1 and enables a lethal-preventing
  board. Also hits the enemy hero for 2 (reach).
- **Truesilver Champion (`CS2_097`)**: equip and attack the same turn. Each attack heals 2 so attacking a minion with
  ≤2 Attack is free. Best targets: minions with ≤4 Health and ≤3 Attack. Do not attack a minion with 4+ Attack when
  your hero is at ≤10 Health unless it prevents lethal. Don't equip a new Truesilver while the current one has
  durability left, unless you will attack with the old one first.
- **Hammer of Wrath (`CS2_094`)**: 3 damage + draw. Target an enemy minion with exactly 3 Health (or ≤3) that
  matters; or the enemy hero for lethal. Fine to cast on turn 4 with nothing else to do.
- **Sen'jin Shieldmasta (`CS2_179`)**: protects your hero and your other minions. Play on an empty or even board.
- **Stampeding Kodo (`NEW1_041`)**: play when at least one enemy minion has ≤2 Attack (it kills a random one).
- **Darkscale Healer (`DS1_055`)**: best when your hero and 2+ minions are damaged.
- **Sunwalker (`EX1_032`)**: the best wall. Play before Tirion to bait silence/transform effects.
- **Cairne Bloodhoof (`EX1_110`)**: two 4/5 bodies; great vs single-target removal.
- **Avenging Wrath (`EX1_384`)**: 8 random 1-damage hits on enemy minions and hero. Best vs several 1–2 Health
  minions or after Equality. Also 8 reach to face when the enemy board is empty.
- **Guardian of Kings (`CS2_088`)**: heal 6 is automatic (to your hero).
- **Lay on Hands (`EX1_354`)**: target your own hero (restore 8) unless you are at full health; draw 3. Play it
  when your hand has ≤2 cards or your health is ≤15.
- **Tirion Fordring (`EX1_383`)**: 6/6 Divine Shield Taunt; when it dies you get a 5/3 Ashbringer weapon. Play on 8
  when the board is stable. Divine Shield: let small minions break it first, don't pop it with your own Pyromancer.
- **Alexstrasza (`EX1_561`)**: choose target: **your own hero if your Health ≤ 12**; otherwise the **enemy hero if
  its Health > 15** (it loses health down to 15); otherwise your own hero if below 15; else the enemy hero.

## 7. Combos, synergies and positioning

- **Wild Pyromancer → Equality** (6 mana): clears every non-Divine-Shield minion on the board, including the Pyro.
  Best when your own board is empty or small.
- **Wild Pyromancer → Consecration** (6 mana): 3 damage to every enemy minion, 2 to the enemy hero; your own
  minions take 1.
- **Equality → Consecration** (8 mana): clears every enemy minion; your minions survive (Consecration hits enemies
  only), at 1 Health.
- **Equality → Avenging Wrath** (10 mana): kills up to 8 enemy minions/hero hits at random.
- **Equality → attack**: one big enemy minion becomes 1 Health; kill it with a 1-attack Recruit or Truesilver.
- **Aldor → Truesilver**: an enemy reduced to 1 Attack can be attacked by your hero for almost free.
- **Divine Shield vs Equality + Pyro**: a Divine Shield minion survives Equality + Pyro (the ping only pops the
  shield). To kill an enemy Divine Shield minion, pop the shield first (attack it with a Recruit, or Pyro +
  another spell earlier in the turn).
- **Positioning:** no card in this deck cares about adjacency. Play minions at the default (rightmost) position.

## 8. Trading rules

- Default stance: **control**. Kill enemy minions before going face as long as the opponent has a board.
- Make a trade when your attacker kills the target and survives, or when the trade is even in mana and the target
  has 3+ Attack.
- Priority of enemy targets: (1) Taunts blocking your lethal; (2) minions that grow or make value every turn (Mana
  Wyrm, Imp Master, Knife Juggler, Flametongue Totem, Cult Master, Questing Adventurer, Gruul, Frothing Berserker,
  Violet Teacher, Hogger, Northshire-type engines); (3) highest-Attack minion; (4) anything that would kill your
  minion next turn.
- Use the weapon on small minions (≤3 Attack) to save your minions for bigger trades.
- Go face only when: the enemy board is empty and you have no better use for the attack; or you have lethal this
  turn; or you have lethal next turn and the enemy cannot kill you next turn (your Health > their visible board
  damage + 6).

## 9. Hero power (Reinforce, 2 mana, summon a 1/1 Recruit)

- Use it whenever 2+ mana is left after your card plays and your board has fewer than 7 minions.
- Spend cards first; Reinforce is the last mana sink. On turn 2 with no 2-drop, Reinforce.
- Recruits are useful: they break Divine Shields, finish 1-Health minions after Equality, and absorb attacks.
- Skip it only when your board already has 3+ small minions and you plan your own Pyromancer + Equality next turn
  (the Recruit would die for nothing); otherwise always use it.

## 10. Lethal checklist (reach)

Count: ready minion attacks not blocked by Taunt + Truesilver 4 (if equipped or equippable this turn) + Ashbringer 5
+ Hammer of Wrath 3 + Consecration 2 + Avenging Wrath up to 8 (only count it fully if the enemy has no minions) +
Alexstrasza (sets enemy to 15, no immediate kill). This deck rarely has burst; most wins are slow board wins.
If lethal exists: clear Taunts first (with spells/weapon), then all attacks to the enemy hero.

## 11. Matchups

**Greedy Mage (Mage midrange: Mana Wyrm, Sorcerer's Apprentice, Frostbolt, Arcane Missiles, Arcane Intellect,
Water Elemental, Fireball, Polymorph, Yeti, Sen'jin, Stranglethorn Tiger, Boulderfist Ogre, Argent Commander,
1 Flamestrike).** Favourable if you keep the board. It plays one greedy action at a time and does not plan.
- Early: kill Mana Wyrm (1/3, grows with spells) and Sorcerer's Apprentice (3/2) on sight: Pyromancer pings,
  Truesilver, trades.
- Mid: Water Elemental 3/6 freezes. Kill it with Truesilver + a minion, or Aldor it and ignore it. Yeti/Sen'jin
  are fine trade targets for Sen'jin/Sunwalker/Kodo bodies.
- Big minions (Boulderfist 6/7, Stranglethorn 5/5 Stealth): Aldor Peacekeeper, or Equality + attacks. Hold one
  Equality for turns 6–8 when two of them are on board.
- Argent Commander (4/2 Charge Divine Shield): break the shield with a Recruit, then kill it.
- 2 Polymorph + 2 Fireball: play Sunwalker and Cairne before Tirion so Polymorph goes on them. Keep health above 10
  (Fireball 6 + Fireblast 1 + Frostbolt 3 = 10 reach from hand).
- Flamestrike (7 mana, 4 to all your minions): from turn 7, don't dump 3+ small minions; Sunwalker's shield
  survives it, Cairne leaves Baine.

**Hunter (Midrange/Face).** Steady Shot deals 2 per turn, so the hunter wins long games if you don't pressure:
stabilize, then attack. Ooze their Eaglehorn Bow / Gladiator's Longbow. Consecration and Pyro answer Unleash the
Hounds (3 mana) boards and Snake Trap snakes. Aldor or Equality on Savannah Highmane (6/5; deathrattle two 2/2s,
Consecration clears them). Explosive Trap pings your attackers when they hit the hero; Freezing Trap returns an
attacking minion. Keep health above ~10 (Kill Command 5 with a Beast).

**Warlock (Zoo).** Wide boards of small minions: Pyromancer, Consecration, Avenging Wrath, Kodo. Kill Knife Juggler,
Imp Master, Dire Wolf Alpha, Flame Imp first. Expect Hellfire and Soulfire (1 mana, 4 dmg). Sen'jin + Farseer stall.
Highly favourable after turn 6.

**Paladin (Aggro/Shockadin or mirror).** Ooze their Truesilver/Light's Justice. Their Divine Shield minions
(Argent Squire, Scarlet Crusader, Silvermoon Guardian) survive Equality + Pyro; pop shields with a Recruit/ping
first. Their Avenging Wrath and Consecration punish many small minions. In the mirror, the one with Tirion later
wins: Aldor their Tirion (it becomes 1/6) instead of attacking into it.

**Warrior (Aggro or Control).** Ooze Fiery War Axe (3 mana) / Arcanite Reaper (5/2) / Arathi Weaponsmith's 2/2.
Kor'kron Elite 4/3 Charge and Frothing Berserker: Aldor, Truesilver. Grommash 4/9 Charge (+6 when damaged):
Aldor Peacekeeper (Attack → 1, its enrage adds back +6 when damaged, so don't damage it after Aldor unless you
kill it) or Equality then kill it. Execute kills damaged minions: don't leave your big minion damaged. Vs Control
Warrior play threats one at a time to beat Brawl.

**Shaman (Midrange).** Kill Flametongue Totem (0/3) first. Feral Spirit wolves (2/3 Taunt): Consecration +
Pyro. Lightning Storm (3, 2–3 to all) punishes small boards. Hex (4) on Tirion: bait it with Sunwalker/Cairne.
Fire Elemental 6/5: Aldor. Ooze Doomhammer. Al'Akir (3/5 Charge DS Taunt Windfury): pop the shield, then
Truesilver/Hammer.

**Rogue (Aggro/Tempo).** Ooze their daggers/Assassin's Blade (Deadly Poison makes the weapon dangerous). Blade
Flurry (4) hits only minions. Kill Edwin VanCleef immediately (Truesilver, Hammer, Aldor). Assassinate (5) and
Sap target Tirion: bait with Sunwalker/Cairne.

**Druid.** Savage Roar (+2 to all their characters) punishes letting them go wide: clear wide boards.
Keeper of the Grove can silence Tirion (no Ashbringer). Big Choose-One minions (Druid of the Claw 4/6,
Ancient of War 5/10 Taunt or 10/5): Aldor and Equality. Swipe kills Recruits.

**Priest.** Slow and weak in this pool. Shadow Word: Death kills Tirion (6 Attack), Mind Control (10) steals it:
play Sunwalker/Cairne first and pressure early. Mass Dispel silences everything.

## 12. Common mistakes

1. Casting Equality without a follow-up (gives the opponent a free trade-in turn against your 1-Health minions).
2. Playing Pyromancer **after** the spell in the same turn (no trigger) or casting a cheap spell with Pyro out
   that kills your own 2-Health minions for nothing.
3. Spending Aldor on a small minion when a 5+ Attack minion is likely next turn (vs Mage/Druid/Warrior).
4. Going face with minions while the opponent has a board that threatens you.
5. Playing Tirion into an untouched Polymorph/Hex/Assassinate when Sunwalker/Cairne could bait first.
6. Attacking a high-Attack minion with Truesilver at low health.
7. Floating mana: if 2 mana is left and the board has room, use Reinforce.
8. Targeting the enemy hero with Alexstrasza when you are at 12 or less.
9. Overextending into Flamestrike (Mage turn 7+) or Consecration/Lightning Storm/Hellfire.
10. Taking too long: in long games keep each decision fast. Don't re-verify obvious plays.

## 13. Script hints (machine-encodable rules)

**Mulligan**
- KEEP always: `NEW1_020`, `EX1_066`, `EX1_096`, `EX1_382`, `EX1_556`, `CS2_117`.
- KEEP one `CS2_097` if hand has a 2- or 3-cost card or going second.
- KEEP one `CS2_093` if opponent class in {WARLOCK, HUNTER, PALADIN, SHAMAN, DRUID, ROGUE} and hand has a 2- or
  3-cost card, or if going second.
- KEEP one `CS2_179` if going second and no other 4-cost kept.
- Max one 4-cost card kept in total. REPLACE: `EX1_619`, `CS2_094`, every card with cost ≥ 5.

**Turn procedure (in this order)**
1. Lethal: if (sum of unblocked ready attacks + weapon attack + direct damage spells castable) ≥ enemy health
   + armor → clear Taunts with spells/weapon, then attack face with everything.
2. AoE: if Pyro (`NEW1_020`) is on board (or castable first) and mana ≥ cost(spell)+(2 if Pyro not yet played):
   - Equality `EX1_619` + Pyro when enemy minions ≥ 2 and sum(enemy minion attack) > sum(own minion attack).
   - Consecration `CS2_093` when it (with Pyro: 3 dmg; without: 2 dmg) kills ≥ 2 enemy minions.
   - Equality + Consecration (8 mana) when enemy minions ≥ 3, or ≥ 2 with any Health ≥ 5.
   - Avenging Wrath `EX1_384` when enemy minions with Health ≤ 2 ≥ 3, or after Equality.
3. Single removal: Aldor `EX1_382` → highest-Attack enemy minion (only if its Attack ≥ 3). Hammer `CS2_094` →
   enemy minion with Health ≤ 3, highest Attack first. Kodo `NEW1_041` only if some enemy minion has Attack ≤ 2.
4. Weapon: equip `CS2_097` if no weapon. Hero attacks the enemy minion with Health ≤ 4 and highest Attack such that
   hero Health after (Health − its Attack + 2) > 10; else face if enemy board empty.
5. Minion attacks: attack where attacker survives and kills target; or trade equal mana when target Attack ≥ 3;
   targets in priority: Taunt (if needed) → engines (Mana Wyrm, Imp Master, Knife Juggler, Flametongue Totem,
   Questing Adventurer, Gruul, Edwin) → highest Attack. Leftover attackers hit face only if enemy board is empty.
6. Spend mana: highest-cost playable minion first (Alexstrasza > Tirion > Guardian > Cairne > Sunwalker > Darkscale
   > Kodo > Sen'jin > Golem/Farseer/Aldor > 2-drops). Lay on Hands `EX1_354` when own Health ≤ 15 or hand ≤ 2.
   Ooze `EX1_066` immediately if enemy has a weapon.
7. Hero power if mana ≥ 2 and own minions < 7.
8. end_turn.

**Targeting rules**
- Heals (`CS2_117`, `EX1_354`): own hero unless own hero is at full health, then the most valuable damaged friendly
  minion.
- Alexstrasza `EX1_561`: own hero if own Health ≤ 12; else enemy hero if enemy Health > 15; else own hero.
- Never give a target to an action whose legal entry lists no targets.

**Never**
- Never cast Equality `EX1_619` when it would kill/weaken more own total stats than enemy, or with no follow-up.
- Never cast a spell with own Pyromancer on board when the ping kills more own minions than enemy minions.
- Never play Pyromancer after the spell you meant to combo with.
- Never attack with the hero into a minion that would leave own hero ≤ 5 Health.
- Never Reinforce on a full board (7 minions).
- Never end the turn with 2+ unused mana and a legal hero power, unless the board is full.

**Face vs trade threshold**
- Trade when enemy has any minion with Attack ≥ 2 or any Taunt; face only when enemy board is empty, or lethal this
  turn, or (lethal next turn AND own Health > enemy board attack + 6).
